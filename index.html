How it works

What a CogniFlo engagement looks like

2 weeks

Diagnose

We pull your event log data and map what actually happens in one end-to-end process. You get the waste quantified in cost and cycle time, and a ranked list of where to start.

8–12 weeks

Redesign

We remove the waste and the variation using Lean and Six Sigma, so the process is stable, capable and actually worth scaling.

Ongoing

Automate & sustain

Only then do we apply RPA, AI and workflow orchestration — and stand up the governance, KPIs and internal capability that keep the gains.
