Core values

The principles that guide the work

Empower Every Voice

The best ideas live closest to the work. We elevate the voices of team members at every level, creating inclusive systems where frontline insight is recognised, rewarded and acted upon.

Lead Through Service

We serve first — clients, teams and community. At CogniFlo, leadership is not about authority but about enabling the success of others. We seek first to understand, and always act in service of delivering value.

Pursue Flow with Purpose

We design systems that flow, so people can thrive. Flow is not just technical efficiency — it's a human enabler. By reducing waste and friction, we unlock creativity, clarity and contribution.

Grow Through Curiosity

Learning is our default setting. We cultivate systems of experimentation, reflection and feedback, where growth is not an event but a way of working.

Read the full background

Guy Desormes is a business transformation executive and Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt with more than two decades leading large-scale performance improvement, mergers and acquisitions, and enterprise operational excellence across manufacturing, life sciences, finance and hospitality.

He has led transformational initiatives at Mylan Pharmaceuticals, Wyndham Worldwide and Bank of America, improving cost, quality and delivery performance while driving organisational alignment and cultural engagement. He played a key role in integration efforts for two of the largest M&A deals in U.S. history — revitalising operational capability, optimising complex supply chains and accelerating post-merger value capture.

Guy is certified and trained by Sigma Breakthrough Technologies, Inc. (SBTI), founded by Six Sigma pioneer Dr. Stephen Zinkgraf, and is recognised for translating advanced CI methodology into practical business outcomes. His career reflects an uncommon combination of Fortune 500 leadership discipline and entrepreneurial execution, further demonstrated through ventures in hospitality, real estate and strategic business advisory.

Today he leads CogniFlo, uniting Lean principles with Industry 4.0 technologies to build intelligent, resilient and continuously improving enterprises — integrating AI, process intelligence, IoT, automation and digital supply chain systems.

Guided by servant leadership and the Shingo principles, Guy believes sustainable transformation is rooted in respect, humility and the empowerment of people to solve problems and grow.
